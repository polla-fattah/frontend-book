# Practical 18 — Make and Defend an Architecture Decision

Related chapter: Chapter 18

## Objective

Create an evidence-backed architecture decision for a product platform without ranking frameworks or adopting complexity by default.

## Stages

1. State product, team, deployment, security, performance, and organizational constraints.
2. Identify at least three plausible options.
3. Record benefits, costs, risks, reversibility, and unknowns.
4. Run one technical spike for the highest-risk unknown.
5. Define validation signals and a review date.
6. Write the decision as an ADR.

## Verification

- The decision follows requirements rather than technology fashion.
- Team boundaries are not treated as browser security boundaries.
- Micro-frontends are considered only when deployment or organizational independence is a real bottleneck.
- The smallest coherent solution is preferred when it satisfies the constraints.

## Extension

Write a reversal plan explaining what evidence would cause the team to revisit the decision.
